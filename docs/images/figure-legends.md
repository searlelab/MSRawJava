# Image notes

how-to-run-mac.png
- compound image, on the mac you need to open the .dmg, but the operating system will block it. Open "Privacy & Security" in "System Settings" and scroll to the bottom. The file should have a button "Open Anyway" (circled in red)

how-to-run-windows.png
- compound image, on Windows, you'll need to click "More info", and then "Run anyway" (both circled in red)

- compound image, showing a-b highlighted. a) VATVSLPR extracted at the MS1 level. b) VATVSLPR extracted at the MS2 level. Useful for checking spray instability, peak width, tailing, etc. MS1/MS2 spectra show highlighted peaks corresponding to the XICs.

raw-file-small-mol-xic.png
- compound image, showing a-b highlighted. a) polysiloxanes eluting across the entire retention time shows consistent atmospheric contamination. XICs generated using chemical structures. b) PEG (polyethylene glycol) XICs showing elution patterns of 415.254, 459.280, 503.306, and 547.332, a common contaminant polymer in samples.
